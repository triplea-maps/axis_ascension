==========================================================
Features
==========================================================

Income is collected before each round.

New factories cannot be built, and captured factories cannot be used.

Transports and submarines follow v3 rules.

Valueless territories have no connections. The Arctic Ocean is impassable beyond Arkhangelsk, and the Leningrad–Baltic connection is also closed.

Sicily and Naples are connected.

The Soviet Union and Japan are neutral toward each other. The Western Allies and the Soviets cannot land in each other’s territories.

Factories can be bombed up to their territory's IPC value.

Air units get +1 attack/defense vs. surface ships. Destroyers get +3 attack/defense vs. submarines. Battleships get +2 attack/defense vs. surface ships.

Factories, cruisers, and battleships are equipped with their own anti-aircraft guns.

Anti-aircraft guns and factories can fire at enemy planes flying over their territory.

Anti-aircraft guns are destroyed upon capture.

Shore bombardment is limited to one shot per landing ground unit, and casualties from naval bombardment may return fire.

You can still collect income and mobilize units even after losing your capital.
